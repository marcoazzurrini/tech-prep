Write `nextWeek(date)`. It returns a new date, seven days later on the calendar, at the same clock time in the zone of the computer. The date it is given must not change.

The week may hold a change of the clocks for daylight saving time.

Example 1: nextWeek(new Date(2024, 2, 28, 9, 30))  ->  a date on 4 April 2024 at 09:30
Example 2: nextWeek(new Date(2024, 11, 28, 10, 0))  ->  a date on 4 January 2025 at 10:00
