Other files should see `sumPrices` under the name `total`, and `countOver` under the name `countAbove`.

Export the two functions under those names. Do not change the functions.

Example 1: total([2, 3])  ->  5
Example 2: countAbove([1, 5, 9], 4)  ->  2
Example 3: Object.keys(exports)  ->  ["countAbove", "total"]
