Write `sinceLabel(minutes)`. It says how long ago something happened, in American English (`en-US`).

Under 60 minutes, count in minutes. Under one day, count in whole hours. Otherwise count in whole days. Round down.

Example 1: sinceLabel(5)  ->  "5 minutes ago"
Example 2: sinceLabel(150)  ->  "2 hours ago"
Example 3: sinceLabel(4000)  ->  "2 days ago"

Note: `minutes` is a whole number, 1 or more.
