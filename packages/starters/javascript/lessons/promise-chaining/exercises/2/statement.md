Write `cityOf(userId)`. It returns a promise of the name of the city the user lives in.

`findUser(id)` gives a promise of the user, who has a `cityId`. `findCity(cityId)` gives a promise of the city's name. Both are in the starting code.

Example 1: cityOf(1)  ->  a promise of "Lyon"
Example 2: cityOf(2)  ->  a promise of "Oslo"
