# Title to Slug (medium)

Write `titleToSlug(title)`. A slug is the title in small letters, with its words joined by `-`.

A word is a run of the letters `a` to `z` and the digits `0` to `9`, in any case. Every other character only separates words.

Example 1: titleToSlug("Hello, World!")  ->  "hello-world"
Example 2: titleToSlug("  Top 10 Tips -- for 2026  ")  ->  "top-10-tips-for-2026"
Example 3: titleToSlug("!!!")  ->  ""
